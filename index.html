Megan Duell

Director, Personal Trainer

Fourteen years at Revolve, and the person you want beside you after an injury.

Megan has spent 14 years at Revolve and brings a Bachelor of Health Science and a Postgraduate Diploma in Exercise Science to every session. She works across musculoskeletal and cardiac rehabilitation, strength and conditioning, and general fitness. If you are coming back from an injury, or you have been told to be careful, she is the one to see.

07

Rob Burns

Personal Trainer

Sixty one, ACSM certified, and he has climbed his own mountain.

Rob is 61, Wellington based and internationally certified by the American College of Sports Medicine. He had a major stroke in 1999 and has trained through it, which is why he is unusually good with clients who have their own mountain to climb. He will tell you the private gym is the point: no memberships, no crowds, no waiting for equipment.

  • Stroke recovery
  • Adaptive training
  • Older adults
Qualifications
American College of Sports Medicine, certified
